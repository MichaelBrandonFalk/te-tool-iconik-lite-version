V1.4 SVOD Checks

This lite version follows the metadata-visible TE Tool SVOD checks: lowercase .mov, codec tag apch, video bit rate at least 145 Mb/s, 1920x1080, 16:9, frame rate rounded to 23.98 or 29.97 fps, 4:2:2 chroma, progressive scan, PCM audio, expected PCM bit rate, 48 kHz, and 24-bit audio. Checks that require sampling media are skipped.
